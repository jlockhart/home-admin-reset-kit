Home Admin Reset Kit

Turn messy home paperwork into one calm digital filing cabinet.

A practical folder structure, naming system, index spreadsheet, and yearly checklist for ordinary bills, receipts, tax PDFs, insurance docs, warranties, manuals, legal documents, and other important files.

No new app. No complicated productivity system. Not an AI product. Just a simple private filing system you can use yourself, or work through with your own AI assistant, in Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, Finder, a NAS, or other storage.

Trial note: v0 is free while the paid-download test is being set up. The intended first paid test is a low-price digital download, likely around NZD $9–$19, once a payment account/link is available.
Please do not send private documents. Do not upload, paste, or send private documents, account numbers, IDs, policy documents, tax records, legal papers, receipts, or financial information. Feedback should only describe the problem in general terms.
